# Decision

## Necessity gates

| Gate | Basis | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Concrete problem exists | fact | The household manually checks Calendar, shared tasks, and notes with no reliable morning digest. |
| Core function addresses it | inference | A digest and review-first assistant directly reduce cross-app checking and missed actions. |
| Delivery form fits | inference | Email, private Discord, or dashboard can deliver a short scheduled briefing to four people. |
| Current alternatives leave trouble | fact + verified research | Cozi, Plamily, and Fami cover shared organization, but privacy boundaries, weekly source synthesis, or reviewable writes remain incomplete or unverified. |
| New build beats adoption | inference | A close self-hosted implementation exists, making adaptation more economical than a greenfield platform. |

## Recommendation

**Adapt**

paperr is the strongest reusable option: its Apache 2.0 self-hosted app already includes local AI, private-by-default spaces, scheduled Morning Brief/Bulletin Board agents, and approval-gated proposed actions. Adapt it to the household's Google Calendar and task sources, structured budget/maintenance inputs, meeting-note scope, and explicit per-person filtering.

The largest remaining unknown is whether those exact Google integrations and visibility rules work without substantial changes, and whether a local model parses dates, owners, and visibility reliably enough for this household.

Reusable option: **paperr** — [GitHub](https://github.com/biswasprateek/paperr), [project site](https://paperr.ai).
